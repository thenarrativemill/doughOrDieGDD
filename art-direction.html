Art Direction

Visual identity, mood boards, style, and color palette

◆ Overview

The original art direction of the project was frankly undefined. The style has always been painterly with some visible stroke variation. However, a definitive target was needed to keep textures cohesive and give the game an identity.

The direction chosen follows a muted color palette that gives old bakery vibes. Chunky assets lend themselves to being seen from an isometric view. Consistent light edgewear on all curvature adds detail to the simpler stylized textures. The flatness of the materials (lack of PBR) brings home the more stylized look without accidentally veering into stylized realism.
