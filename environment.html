◆ Bakery (Day)
Day Environment

The bakery is the player's home base during daytime hours. It features warm lighting, wooden counters, and shelves of ingredients. The layout supports the crafting and ordering loop. Mostly desaturated colors, round and chunky shape language — inviting and warm.

◆ Arena (Night)
Night Environment

The arena is where combat takes place each night. Darker and more foreboding in tone, with torch lighting and crumbling stone architecture contrasting against the warmth of the bakery.
