Gameplay

Mechanics, player goals & gameplay loop

◆ Gameplay Overview

The player begins in the bakery in the prep phase. This allows the player to prepare up to 3 dough bases. A dough base consists of flour and water OR milk. To start a dough base, the player grabs flour, adds water, then places that dough base into the mixer. When finished, they bring it to a countertop and add the remaining ingredients for the chosen recipe.

Once the bakery opens, customers line up at the front register with a patience bar for being attended to. After taking their order, an icon of the pastry they'd like appears above their head with a new patience bar. Players must balance serving customers while also creating their bread golem for that night's fight.

Golem recipes always require five of the same base recipe dough balls placed into slots in the walk-in oven. Your golem can have a maximum of two special abilities — one per arm slot. The modifier dough determines the ability. The day ends when all customers are served, the walk-in oven door is closed, and the player presses the oven dial on the wall.

◆ Mechanics
  • Baking: Combine ingredients and complete minigames to create an end product
  • Time Management: Manage time between tending to customers' orders and baking your Pastry Golem
  • Combat: First-person fighting with character-centric abilities (90% melee, 10% ranged)
◆ Short, Mid & Long-Term Goals
Prep Phase
  • Short: Create dough bases
  • Mid: Get ahead of customers' orders to manage customers and bake the golem later
  • Long: Win the golem fight, unlock new recipes and ingredients, upgrade bakery
Open Hours — Baking Loop
  • Short: Bake customer orders and golem doughs
  • Mid: Create the golem you need for the fight later
  • Long: Win the golem fight, unlock new recipes and ingredients, upgrade bakery
Open Hours — Customer Loop
  • Short: Tend to customers' orders in a reasonable time
  • Mid: Gain momentum/renown to spend or gain aid for both bakery and arena
  • Long: Win the golem fight, unlock new recipes and ingredients, upgrade bakery
Fighting Phase
  • Short: Win the fight
  • Mid: Gain a reward from the Pick Three
  • Long: Win the golem fight, unlock new recipes and ingredients, upgrade bakery
