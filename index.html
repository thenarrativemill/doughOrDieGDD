Game Core
Overview

In a world where your finest ingredients are either your greatest profit or your deadliest weapon, every recipe is a choice between a thriving shop and a winning fighter.

Players manage baking for customers and bake their bread golems during the day, then fight other bakers' golems in the Dough Brawl Arena after closing.

View in Overview ›
The 3 C's
Overview

Character: Francesca "Fran" Panettiere — 5'3", warm and protective, third-generation immigrant raised by her Nonna in the bakery.

Camera: Isometric Top-Down in the bakery, First-Person in the arena.

Controls: Bakery and arena controller maps to be added.

Audience & Playstyle
Overview

Target audience: teen+, casual and core gamers, streamers, and people with interests in baking, mechs, fighting games, and sims.

Playstyle targets the Mastermind archetype and Challenge, Fantasy, and Expression pleasures from LeBlanc's taxonomy.

Market Survey
Overview

Comparable titles: Dave the Diver, Papa's Games, Potion Craft, and Titanfall 2. Dough or Die differentiates through its baking-as-combat mechanic and community-rooted brand identity.

Mechanics
Gameplay

Three core mechanics drive the game: Baking (combine ingredients via minigames), Time Management (balance customers and golem prep), and Combat (first-person fighting, 90% melee).

Player Goals
Gameplay

Each phase has layered short, mid, and long-term goals. Short-term goals keep the player focused moment-to-moment; all long-term goals converge on winning the nightly golem fight and unlocking new recipes and upgrades.

Gameplay Loop
Gameplay

The loop cycles through four phases each day: prep (bake dough bases), open hours (serve customers and bake golem), close (select loadout), and the nightly arena fight followed by a Pick Three reward screen.

A lineage of bakers with the Gift of Animation has secretly run the Dough Brawl Arena for generations. Now the legacy falls to Fran, who must carry on her Nonna's championship while keeping the bakery alive.

Main Character
Characters

Francesca "Fran" Panettiere — 5'3", brown curly hair, olive skin. Warm, loud, and protective. Grew up in the bakery, sings to her golems while baking them, goes stone-faced the moment the fight begins.
