◆ Ingredients

Base Ingredients:

FlourWaterMilkButterOilSugarSaltEggsYeast

Special Ingredients — when added to your pastry golem, you get a different combat ability:

  • Chocolate: Leap forward and smash the ground releasing a shockwave of chocolate shards and briefly stunning the enemy.
  • Cream Cheese: Adds a shield ability that absorbs incoming damage and reflects a percentage back to the attacker — like thorns.
  • Almonds: Charge a heavy uppercut that increases attack damage briefly and knocks back enemies.
  • Blueberries: Fire a rapid spread of blueberry projectiles that arc slightly and splatter on impact, damaging enemies in a small area.

◆ Reputation System

There are two types of reputation: Momentum (Daily) and Renown (Permanent).

Momentum reflects how well you serve customers during the day. It is earned through efficiency and quality of performance and resets at the start of each new day. At the end of the day, Momentum grants immediate advantages in the upcoming golem fight — extra ability charges, reduced cooldowns, temporary stat boosts, and bonus combat modifiers.

Renown is the cumulative reputation you gain throughout your entire playthrough. It unlocks persistent bakery-focused upgrades: low-level automation, access to higher-quality ingredients, and bakery safety nets that soften mistakes.

Your daily Momentum is graded on a scale of: Terrible → Poor → Okay → Great → Excellent (0–4 points). Winning an arena fight nets 6 renown points — 1.5x the maximum daily bakery performance.

◆ Customer Ordering System

Day one starts with 3 customers. Each day a random growth percentage between 8–15% is chosen. Once the customer count reaches 18 or higher, growth plateaus. In late game, the daily count is randomly chosen between 18–25 customers.

Starting Day 3, a randomly chosen recipe forces 60% of customers to order it. The other 40% order randomly. When the first special ingredient is unlocked, customers have a 10% chance to request it with their order. This increases to 15% after the second unlock, and 25% after the third.

Exceptions — these recipes require their special ingredient and are exempt from optional adds:

  • Babka: Chocolate (required)
  • Brownies: Chocolate (required)
  • Cinnamon Roll: Cream Cheese (required)
  • Cannoli: Cream Cheese (required)

◆ Customer Grading System

When customers walk up to your counter they have a patience bar for being attended to. After taking their order, a new patience bar begins for how long it takes to receive the order. The bar dwindles as you work. Once you give them their order, the bar stops — the percentage remaining is your score for their patience.

◆ Combat System

The modular combat system is split into three actor components (AC):

  • Combat AC: Animations, bones (to know the hit result), and damage being afflicted.
  • Health AC: Health information, UI, and hit react animations.
  • Hit Stop AC: Applies time dilation when you or the enemy are struck for visual feedback.
